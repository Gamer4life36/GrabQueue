GrabQueue - Supported Sites
============================

auto-updated, so this list grows over time. Generated from the bundled
engine versions. Anything with a direct file link (mp4, mp3, jpg, ...)
works too, even if the site is not listed.

DRM-protected services (Netflix, Disney+, Spotify, ...) are not supported.
